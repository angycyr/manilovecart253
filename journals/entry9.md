for this final project, i think it would be very fun to make a talisman making simulator. <br>

traditional daoist talisman (the main inspiration) are composed of a long yellow parchment with red cinnabar ink (or black soot ink for lettering and red ink seals for marks of ownership). visually, it mixes both elements of symetry on a vertical axis and hanzi. <br>

with this in mind, i will start my project with creating a 1280 by 720 fully yellow canvas. when right mouse button is clicked, a red circle will be drawn on the mouse position. when left button is clicked, a red circle will be drawn on both the mouse position and it's vertical counter part. these two mechanics will be the basis for talisman making. <br>

other features i want to include: <br>
    - a quick restart button instead of an undo button, ink on paper is a very unforgiving medium, so the simulation should mimic that <br>
    - a png saving button because i want to be able to print out my talisman and put them on my walls. <br>
    - ink thickness based on the speed of the mouse to mimic brush strokes. (or maybe transparency?) <br>
    - sounds
